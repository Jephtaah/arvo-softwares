# Voice and Tone Guidelines

## Overview
Arvo Reach uses a warm, friendly, and plain-spoken voice to create a calm and trustworthy experience for non-technical users.

## Voice Principles

### Warm and Friendly
- Use language that feels welcoming and approachable
- Avoid cold, technical, or robotic terminology
- Favor words that create emotional connection

### Plain and Clear
- Prefer simple, everyday words over jargon or acronyms
- Example: "Daily sending limit" instead of "throttle"
- Example: "Paused to protect your inbox" instead of "circuit breaker tripped"
- Avoid unnecessary complexity in communication

### Calm and Reassuring
- Use language that reduces anxiety rather than increases it
- Frame limitations as protections rather than restrictions
- Focus on what users can do, not what they can't do

## Language Patterns

### Preferred Terms
| Instead of... | Use... |
|---------------|---------|
| throttle | daily sending limit |
| circuit breaker tripped | paused to protect your inbox |
| utilization | use |
| leverage | use |
| facilitate | help |
| implement | set up |
| configure | adjust |
| parameters | settings |
| credentials | login info |
| authentication | sign in |
| authorization | permission |

### Sentence Structure
- Use active voice when possible
- Keep sentences short and direct
- Use positive framing ("You can...") over negative framing ("You cannot...")
- Explain the "why" behind limitations and requirements

### Tone Characteristics
- Friendly but not overly casual
- Professional but not stiff
- Helpful but not pushy
- Confident but not arrogant
- Empathetic but not patronizing

## Application Areas
These voice and tone guidelines apply to:
- User interface text (buttons, labels, messages, etc.)
- Error messages and validation
- Onboarding flows
- Help text and tooltips
- Email templates and notifications
- Documentation and support materials
- Marketing and promotional content

## Examples

### Good Examples
- "Your daily sending limit helps protect your email reputation"
- "We paused sending to keep your inbox healthy"
- "Choose how often you want to receive updates"
- "Let's set up your email signature together"

### Avoid
- "Rate limit exceeded"
- "System blocked transmission due to policy violation"
- "You must configure SMTP settings"
- "Authorization token expired"
